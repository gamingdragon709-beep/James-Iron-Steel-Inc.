## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an industrial-luxury visual language tailored for high-end architectural ironwork, bespoke structural steel, and master metal fabrication. It merges the gravitas of multi-generational industrial endurance (established in 1952) with the refined elegance demanded by world-class architects, general contractors, and discerning private estates.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Industrial Luxury / Architectural Monumentalism**:
- **Tension of opposites:** Immense, structural deep-charcoal massing set against warm sandstone planes and polished burnished brass accents.
- **Rhythmic cadence:** Alternating high-contrast structural blocks—transitioning from obsidian blackwork (#0F1115) into warm limestone/sandstone exhibition fields (#F8F7F4)—evoking raw iron, foundry sparks, and refined architectural installations.
- **Structural precision:** Monospaced and condensed index numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`), architectural hairline dividers, precision badge pills, and monumental hero typography mirroring the strength of structural I-beams and custom railings.
- **Emotional resonance:** Unshakable permanence, generational craftsmanship, unapologetic luxury, and technical masterwork.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the forge, raw structural steel alloys, and luxury patinas:

- **Primary (`#C5A059` — Foundry Bronze):** Used for focal CTA buttons, high-priority interactive highlights, glowing badges, and index numbers. Accompanied by `#D4AF37` (Burnished Gold) for subtle hover states and metallic gradient transitions.
- **Secondary (`#7E8691` — Steel Slate / Graphite):** Mid-tone industrial steel tone for secondary labels, technical specs, subdued borders, and icon fills. Deep slate counterpart is `#2A2E35` for inner structural borders and subtle card strokes.
- **Neutrals Dark:**
  - `#0F1115` (Cast Iron): Master canvas background, grounding sections in heavy structural black.
  - `#16181D` (Rolled Steel): Surface-raised cards, floating toolbars, and elevated containers.
  - `#1F232B` (Billet Grey): Elevated interactive states and card hover planes.
- **Neutrals Light / Sandstone Contrast:**
  - `#F8F7F4` (Limestone Sandstone): The alternate full-width canvas background creating dramatic rhythmic alternation across narrative bands.
  - `#EFECE6` (Honed Travertine): Nested card surfaces within light sections.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - High-contrast pure `#FFFFFF` and `#F5F5F7` for crisp dark-mode legibility.
  - `#111317` for crisp typography over light sandstone fields.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances three architectural disciplines:
- **Structural Display (`Syne`):** Imparts brutalist geometry, confident presence, and heavyweight industrial power across primary titles and section headings.
- **Editorial Sub-Narrative (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for prestige quotes, historic milestones (e.g., "Founded in 1952"), and customer acclaim, infusing bespoke old-world craftsmanship.
- **Industrial Telemetry & Eyebrows (`Oswald`):** All-caps condensed lettering used for section tracking labels, badge tags, navigation items, CTA buttons, and sequential counters (`01`, `02`, `03`).
- **Body & Legibility (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, balanced, modern grotesque that provides effortless reading across lengthy project scopes, specifications, and commercial estimates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column architectural grid with dynamic visual pacing:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, max container width 1280px, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer section margin `margin: 4rem`. Full-bleed sections stretch 100vw to allow dramatic alternation between dark `#0F1115` metal forge views and bright `#F8F7F4` sandstone gallery showcases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, margins 2.5rem. Three-column project grids collapse to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Sequential callouts stack into full-width cards.

### Vertical Section Rhythms
Sections alternate between **Dark Monolith** (`#0F1115` cast iron canvas) and **Sandstone Quarry** (`#F8F7F4` honed light canvas). Each section features standard vertical padding of `6rem` (desktop) and `3.5rem` (mobile), creating clear, rhythmic breaks between heritage storytelling, service capabilities, project showcases, and conversion forms.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and physical material transitions rather than artificial drop shadows:

1. **Foundry Surface Tiers (Dark Theme):**
   - **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#0F1115`
   - **Mid Tier (Cards, Feature Modules):** `#16181D` with subtle 1px border `rgba(126, 134, 145, 0.16)`
   - **Lifted Tier (Floating Nav, Modals, Forms):** `#1F232B` backed by a crisp, low-intensity ambient glow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.22)`.

2. **Sandstone Surface Tiers (Light Theme):**
   - **Ground Tier:** `#F8F7F4`
   - **Raised Cards:** `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `rgba(42, 46, 53, 0.08)` and subtle architectural drop: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.05)`.

3. **Metallic Hairlines:**
   Precision 1px dividers utilize a linear gradient (`transparent`, `#C5A059`, `transparent`) to simulate light catching the beveled edge of milled steel.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Level 1 (Soft / Architectural Precision)**:
- Core UI elements (cards, containers, inputs) use strict `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radiuses, reflecting the machined edges of cold-rolled steel and custom plate fabrications.
- Project media cards feature crisp, geometric 4px corners to maintain architectural discipline.
- **Pill Exception:** Status tags, tracking badges, and sequential callout markers (`01`, `02`) utilize pill geometries (`9999px`) to create an immediate, tactile contrast against the monolithic grid lines.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Industrial CTA:** Background `#C5A059` with text `#0F1115`, uppercase `Oswald` lettering, tracked out (`0.14em`), padding `1rem 2rem`, 4px radius. On hover, background shifts to `#D4AF37` with an ambient warm bronze outer aura (`0 0 20px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Steel Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#7E8691`, text `#FFFFFF` or `#0F1115` (context-dependent), padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. On hover: border `#C5A059`, text `#C5A059`.
- **Text Link with Arrow:** Uppercase Oswald label with inline directional brass arrow (`→`), transitioning with a 4px rightward slide on hover.

### 2. Sequential Value Callouts (01, 02, 03)
- Rectangular slate-backed cards (`#16181D` over dark or `#FFFFFF` over light) with a 2-column or 3-column distribution.
- **Index Tag:** Numeric counter styled in `Oswald` (`#C5A059` bronze, 32px), vertically paired with an architectural headline (`Syne` 18px) and slate body copy.
- Separated by hairline borders (`#2A2E35`).

### 3. Service & Architectural Project Cards
- **Image Header:** High-aspect ratio architectural imagery (16:10 or 4:3) with a subtle zoom scale on hover (1.04x ease-out).
- **Body Panel:** Clean card base featuring a pill badge (e.g., "STRUCTURAL IRONWORK", "EST. 1952"), followed by an uppercase title, descriptive brief, and a dedicated bronze "View Engineering Specs →" link.

### 4. Testimonial & Acclaim Cards
- Styled with high-contrast surfaces, a 5-star metallic bronze rating row (`#C5A059`), italicized quote body styled in editorial typography, and signature block detailing client credentials (e.g., "Lead Principal, Studio V Architects").

### 5. High-Converting Estimate & Quote Form
- Monolithic form container housed within `#16181D` (dark) or crisp `#FFFFFF` (sandstone) card with 1px border.
- **Form Inputs:** Height `52px`, background `#0F1115` / `#F8F7F4`, border `1px solid #2A2E35`, text `Plus Jakarta Sans`, active focus state ring `1px solid #C5A059`.
- **Select Dropdowns:** Custom styled with brass chevron iconography.
- **Submit Module:** Full-width primary bronze button anchored at the bottom.

### 6. Badges & Architectural Pill Indicators
- Compact height (`26px`), uppercase 11px `Oswald`, tracking `0.18em`.
- Dark Mode: Subtle bronze background `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`, 1px border `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`, text `#C5A059`.
- Light Mode: Neutral slate background `rgba(42, 46, 53, 0.06)`, text `#16181D`.
