FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you provide free on-site estimates? +
Yes, complimentary estimates are standard at James Iron & Steel. We can evaluate your architectural drawings, sketches, or come directly to your job site in Charlotte, Monroe, or the surrounding region to take exact laser measurements and discuss material specs.
What is your typical turnaround time for fabrication? +
Lead times vary depending on project complexity. Standard residential railings and gates typically require 2 to 3 weeks from approved shop drawings to final install. Large structural packages or custom powder-coated automated entry gates are scheduled according to contractor milestones.
Do you handle both commercial and residential projects? +
Yes. We have dedicated teams for both. Our commercial division fabricates structural steel beams, fire escape stairs, and ADA railings, while our residential team focuses on high-finish interior handrails, drive gates, and decorative accents.
What warranties do you provide on your metalwork? +
We stand behind every weld with a written 1-Year Workmanship Warranty. Additionally, our architectural powder-coat finishes feature manufacturer warranties against chalking, chipping, and peeling.
Can you match existing architectural iron designs or sketches? +
Absolutely. Our craftsmen have 70+ years of collective lineage replicating historical wrought iron, matching existing balustrades, or building modern minimalist profiles from architect CAD files and Pinterest inspiration photos.
How durable is the finish against rust in coastal areas? +
For coastal jobs in Oak Island, Wilmington, or high-humidity lakefronts, we utilize hot-dip galvanization or marine-grade aluminum coupled with multi-coat electrostatic powder coating to ensure lifetime rust inhibition.
